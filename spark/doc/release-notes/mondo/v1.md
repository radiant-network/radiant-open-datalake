## [1.0] - 2026-08-05

### Added

- Initial contract. The `normalized_mondo` dataset family, narrowed to MAJOR 1, is published to table
  `mondo_v1`, one row per Mondo `[Term]`, parsed from the versioned `mondo-base.obo` release named by the
  `--version` launch argument.
- Columns: `id` (e.g. `MONDO:0000001`), `name`, `parents` (direct `is_a` ids), and `alt_ids` (the
  `alt_id` tags).

### Notes

- Parsing is delegated to Ferlab `obo-parser` `1.3.1` (`downloadOntologyData`); the normalizer only
  flattens its `OntologyTerm` into the columns above.
- **Fields not carried, by limitation of `obo-parser` 1.3.1:** it extracts no `def`/`synonym`/`xref`, and
  has no `is_obsolete` handling — so obsolete terms are ingested but not distinguished. Restoring an
  obsolescence flag needs an upstream `obo-parser` release or a small supplement here.
- **Direct parents only.** Transitive ancestor closure, `is_leaf` and top-node filtering exist in
  `obo-parser`'s Spark ancestor pipeline but are deliberately out of scope for this normalized table —
  they belong to a downstream enriched job.
- Only `[Term]` stanzas with an uppercase-prefixed `id` survive; `[Typedef]` and header stanzas parse to
  an empty id and are dropped.
