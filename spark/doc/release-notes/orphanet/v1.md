## [1.0] - 2026-08-12

### Added

- Initial contract. The `normalized_orphanet` dataset family, narrowed to MAJOR 1, is published to
  table `orphanet_v1` from the Orphadata **product6** (`en_product6.xml`, gene-disorder associations)
  and **product9_ages** (`en_product9_ages.xml`, disorder ages of onset/death and inheritance) files of
  the release named by the `--version` launch argument.
- Both XML products are landed in the versioned raw prefix by the manual download DAG (the operator
  supplies the Orphadata base URL); the job reads each through `RawInput.readVersioned`, substituting
  `{{VERSION}}`, and parses the XML in-memory (`scala.xml`).
- product6 is exploded to one row per gene-disorder-locus association, carrying the disorder identity,
  the gene identity and its external cross-references (Ensembl, Genatlas, HGNC, OMIM, Reactome,
  SwissProt), the association type/status and the gene locus.
- product9 contributes `average_age_of_onset`, `average_age_of_death` and `type_of_inheritance` arrays,
  left-joined onto the associations by `orpha_code`.
