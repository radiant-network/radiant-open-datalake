# Changelog — clinvar_rcv contract MAJOR 1

All notable changes to the `clinvar_rcv` data contract, lineage `1.x`, will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/2.0.0/), and this contract
adheres to the lineage rule of [SJRA-1546]: MINOR is bumped in place for an additive change carried by
schema evolution, a breaking change is a new MAJOR with its own changelog file.

## Scope

This is the **RCV** half of the monthly [ClinVar XML release][clinvar-xml]
(`ClinVarRCVRelease_YYYY-MM.xml.gz`, `version` = `YYYY-MM`), not the ClinVar VCF that the separate
`clinvar` contract normalizes. The two are different products and neither supersedes the other:

- `clinvar_v1` is keyed by **locus** and carries the aggregated `CLNSIG` only.
- `clinvar_rcv_v1` is keyed by **RCV accession** — one aggregated (variant, condition) assertion — and
  is the only release that exposes the **per-submitter (SCV) classifications** behind it.

A variant asserted against three conditions therefore has one row in `clinvar_v1` and three here.

## [1.0] - 2026-09-25

### Added

- Initial contract. The `normalized_clinvar_rcv` dataset family, narrowed to MAJOR 1, is published to
  table `clinvar_rcv_v1`, one row per `<ClinVarSet>` of the RCV release named by the `--version` launch
  argument.
- `accession` and `version` — the RCV accession and its version, from the aggregate record's
  `ClinVarAccession`.
- `clinvar_id` — the ClinVar variation id, from the `MeasureSet` of type `Variant`. Compound
  heterozygotes and diplotypes carry a `GenotypeSet` instead, whose nested measure sets are searched in
  document order. Null when the record describes something other than a variant (a gene, an OMIM
  record).
- `clinical_significance` — the aggregate germline classification as an array. ClinVar joins co-asserted
  classifications with `/` and `;` (`Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic`), and each part is published
  separately. Null, not an empty array, when the record carries no germline classification.
- `date_last_evaluated` (date) and `submission_count`, from the aggregate `Description` attributes.
- `review_status` and `review_status_stars`, the [documented star rating][review-status] of that status:
  4 `practice guideline`, 3 `reviewed by expert panel`, 2 `criteria provided, multiple submitters, no
  conflicts`, 1 `criteria provided, single submitter` and `criteria provided, conflicting
  classifications`, 0 for every other status and for a record with none.
- `traits` — the preferred name of every trait the record was asserted against, in document order.
- `origins` — the distinct allele origins (`germline`, `somatic`, `de novo`, …) observed across the
  record's `ObservedIn` samples, sorted.
- `submissions` — the SCV submissions the record aggregates, one struct each: `submitter`, `scv`,
  `version`, `review_status`, `review_status_stars`, `clinical_significance` (the submitted germline
  classification, unsplit), `date_last_evaluated`.
- `clinical_significance_count` — how many submissions back each part of `clinical_significance`, e.g.
  `{"Pathogenic": 3, "Likely pathogenic": 1}`. A submission whose classification ClinVar did not carry
  into the aggregate is absent from the map rather than counted at zero.

### Not in this MAJOR

- **Germline classifications only.** `Classifications` also admits `SomaticClinicalImpact`,
  `OncogenicityClassification` and `NoClassification`; none is read, and none is published. Adding them
  is additive and belongs in a MINOR.
- **No locus columns**, and so no `locus_hash`. An RCV record names a variant by ClinVar id, and the
  coordinates live on the `Measure/SequenceLocation` subtree this contract does not extract. Join
  through `clinvar_id` for now.
