## [1.0] - 2026-07-29

### Added

- Initial contract. `normalized_clinvar` is published to `opendatalake.reference.clinvar`, one row per
  locus, from the versioned ClinVar VCF release named by the `--version` launch argument.
- Locus columns `chromosome`, `start`, `end`, `name`, `reference`, `alternate`.
- ClinVar INFO fields, unescaped and lowercased.
- `clin_sig`, `clin_sig_conflict` and `clnrevstat` as arrays, split on `|` after the ClinVar
  separators (`^_`, `|_`, `/`) and the conflict counts (`(\d{1,2})`) are normalized away.
- `clndisdb`, `clndisdbincl`, `clndnincl` and `mc` as arrays, and `conditions` from `clndn` with
  underscores restored to spaces.
- `interpretations`, the distinct union of `clin_sig` and `clin_sig_conflict` collected per locus.
- `inheritance`, decoded from the ClinVar `origin` bitmask (`germline`, `somatic`, `de-novo`, … and
  `unknown` when absent).

### Removed

- `clin_sig_original` and `clndn` are dropped from the published table; `clin_sig` and `conditions`
  supersede them.
