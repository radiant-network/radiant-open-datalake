## [1.0] - 2026-08-07

### Added

- Initial contract. The `normalized_spliceai` dataset family, narrowed to MAJOR 1, is published to
  table `spliceai_v1` from the SpliceAI precomputed delta-score VCFs named by the `--version` launch
  argument. Illumina distributes the scores as two VCFs (SNV and indel) with identical schemas; the raw
  glob (`spliceai_scores.raw.*.hg38.vcf.gz`) unions both into one table, one row per variant-gene pair.
- Locus columns `chromosome`, `start`, `end`, `reference`, `alternate`.
- `allele` and `symbol` from the `SpliceAI` INFO field.
- Delta scores `ds_ag`, `ds_al`, `ds_dg`, `ds_dl` (acceptor/donor gain/loss, doubles) and their
  positions `dp_ag`, `dp_al`, `dp_dg`, `dp_dl` (ints), split out of the pipe-delimited `SpliceAI` field.
- `max_score` — a struct `{ ds, type }` holding the strongest of the four delta scores for the row and
  the event(s) that reached it (ties keep every tied event; a zero score yields a null `type`).
